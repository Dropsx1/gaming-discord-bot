# 📜 Universal Play Haven Rules

1. **Be kind.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks.
2. **Keep it SFW.** No NSFW, gore, or shock content anywhere, including avatars and names.
3. **No spam or self-promo** outside the channels made for it. No unsolicited DMs or ads.
4. **No cheating talk.** Don't share cheats, exploits, account trading, or pirated games.
5. **Keep it on-topic.** Use the right channel, and keep drama out of public chat.
6. **Respect privacy.** Never share anyone's personal info, including your own.
7. **AI content is welcome,** but label it, credit sources, and don't pass off others' work as yours.
8. **Follow Discord's rules:** https://discord.com/terms and https://discord.com/guidelines
9. **Listen to staff.** Questions or reports? Open a ticket instead of arguing in chat.
